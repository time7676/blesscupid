Iconography doctrine — what the brand refuses, and why

Sourced from BLE-108 pastor-mode review (Pastor sign-off, CEO doctrinal positioning). Six glyphs are off the table forever, not just for pastor mode and not just for v1. The approved set is the affirmative vocabulary. Refusing one to use the other is the load-bearing decision — future Designer work cannot reintroduce a rejected glyph without re-litigating the Holy Code of Conduct.

Approved · 3 glyphs · the affirmative vocabulary
Diterima

Tongkat gembala

Shepherd-staff. Pastoral metaphor by direct etymology — the staff is what a pastor (Latin pastor, "shepherd") carries.

"Gada-Mu dan tongkat-Mu, itulah yang menghibur aku." — Mazmur 23:4 (TB2).

Diterima

Dua sosok bersisian

Two figures side by side — walking-companion frame. Never authority-over, never one-on-top-of-the-other. Used in mentor / accountability surfaces.

"Bukankah baik dan indah, apabila saudara-saudara diam bersama dengan rukun!" — Mazmur 133:1.

Diterima

Pejalan beriring

Walking-companion: same direction, one slightly behind, never above. The Emmaus posture — Christ walked with the disciples before they recognized Him.

"Bukankah hati kita berkobar-kobar, ketika Ia berbicara dengan kita di tengah jalan…" — Lukas 24:32.

Rejected · 6 glyphs · do not reintroduce
Ditolak · panopticon

Mata (eye)

Reads as panopticon — the watcher whose presence shapes the behavior of the watched. Opposite of shepherding: a shepherd walks alongside, not above.

"Lihatlah, Aku berdiri di muka pintu dan mengetuk." — Wahyu 3:20. Christ knocks; He does not stare in.

Ditolak · investigation

Kaca pembesar (magnifying glass)

Investigation glyph. Implies the user is a case to be solved or evidence to be examined. Pastoral relationships are not investigations and the mentee is not a suspect.

"Hendaklah kamu saling mengaku dosamu dan saling mendoakan, supaya kamu sembuh." — Yakobus 5:16. Mutual confession is healing, not forensics.

Ditolak · carceral

Gembok (lock / security)

Frames the relationship as containment — keeping the user "safe" from themselves. A covenant of trust is not a containment system. Lock semantics also blur with auth/account-security UI elsewhere in the app.

Containment is a prison metaphor. Discipleship is a road metaphor — Lukas 24, the road to Emmaus.

Ditolak · authority armor

Perisai (shield)

Reads as authority defending downward. Positions the operator over the user instead of beside. Visually adjacent to security/admin-panel chrome — wrong frame for a covenant.

Authority that protects from above is not the shepherd model of Mazmur 23 — the shepherd walks the valley with the sheep, not above them.

Ditolak · scoring

Bintang (star / score)

Star, badge, and score iconography (gamification rewards) collapse a covenant relationship into a metric. Avoiding "engagement scores" means avoiding the visual language of scoring entirely.

Spiritual maturity is not a leaderboard. Refuse the ranking shape.

Ditolak · approval gating

Tanda centang "disetujui pendeta"

"Pastor-approved" verification badges frame the user as something to be certified. Two-party consent is approval-of-relationship, not approval-of-person. Removing the certification glyph removes the certification framing.

"…tidak ada laki-laki atau perempuan, karena kamu semua adalah satu di dalam Kristus Yesus." — Galatia 3:28. Approval-of-person creates spiritual-class hierarchy.
