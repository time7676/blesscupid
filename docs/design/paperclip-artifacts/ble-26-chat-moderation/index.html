BLE-26 · Chat moderation banners — three-voice pattern

Source: BLE-31 §10 (pastoral tone reference, draft — Pastor sign-off pending). Three distinct voices for three distinct moments. Cream surface, gold accent, cobalt action; amber for warmth. Red is reserved exclusively for genuine panic-exit and never appears on moderation.

Color ruleCream canvas · gold accent · cobalt action. Amber-warm for compose-time. No red.
Tone rulePlain, adult, kind. Not corporate. Not church-bulletin. Pastor canonical voice from §1–§6.
Door ruleEvery banner must surface a door — appeal path, cooldown info, or restoration step.
BilingualBahasa default. English fallback. Templates verbatim from §1–§6, never paraphrased.
Voice 01 · Compose-time

Friend leans over your shoulder

"Hey — eyes on what you just typed. You sure?"

  • Triggered before send when classifier flags risk (slur token, scam keyword, off-platform push).
  • Yellow-warm — gold-100 → amber-100 gradient. Gold-500 left rule.
  • Never blocks. Always includes "send anyway" path. Friction, not gate.
  • Subject of doctrinal warmth, not corporate compliance ("we noticed", not "violation detected").
