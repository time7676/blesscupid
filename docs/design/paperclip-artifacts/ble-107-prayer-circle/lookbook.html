Iconography sheet

All glyphs are 24×24, stroke-based (1.6), tradition-neutral, and pass the doctrinal red lines. Rejected alternatives are documented at the bottom for the pastoral record so future designers don't reach for them.

Approved · primary identity

community-ring
PRIMARY IDENTITY · feature glyph for Prayer Circle. Three abstract members on a dotted ring. No human silhouette.
clasped-hands · abstract
SECONDARY · two interlocking arcs. Reads "joined" without invoking Dürer praying-hands cliché.
open-palms
SECONDARY · upward arcs cradling a dot. Universal "receive" gesture, charismatic-friendly without privileging.
pray-dot
"Saya doakan" affordance · single lit dot on the dotted ring. Tap = you join the circle of pray-ers for this request.

Rejected · for pastoral record

praying-hands · Dürer
Constraint #1 — evangelical kitsch. Dated to Indonesian young adults. Stock cliché.
kneeling figure
Constraint #2 — privileges one prayer posture (kneeling) over standing/hands-up/prostration. Excludes denominations.
halo + figure
Constraint #4 — halos / rays / iconographic glyphs break the gold-token discipline of the system.
stained-glass cross
Constraint #4 — denominationally over-specific (Catholic/mainline). Excludes Indonesian charismatic.
🙏 as primary chrome
Constraint #3 — in Indonesian context this glyph reads Buddhist/Hindu *sembah* before Christian prayer. Decoration in chat fine; identity glyph not.

Constraints checklist · pastor sign-off

Each binding visual constraint from the issue traced to the surface where it is enforced.

1. No "praying hands" stock cliché. No Dürer silhouette appears on any surface. Identity glyph is `community-ring`. `clasped-hands` glyph is two abstract interlocking arcs, not folded hands.
2. No kneeling-figure silhouettes. All glyphs are abstract / geometric. No human posture is drawn. Tradition-neutral across charismatic (GBI, COOL) and mainline (GKI, Catholic).
3. No 🙏 emoji as primary chrome. 🙏 does not appear in any surface as identity, button, or section header. Documented as rejected on the iconography sheet.
4. No halos, no rays, no stained-glass. Hero artwork is a dotted ring of three nodes. No radiating rays. Gold use is restricted to `gold-100` background tile + `gold-300` left rule on scripture quote, matching token discipline from BLE-22.
5. Consent state visually persistent. Cobalt consent banner pinned at the top of every Prayer Circle surface (home, detail, compose, opt-in/out). Banner names the circle, the audience count, and an `Adjust` link. On the Detail surface the banner identifies the author's chosen visibility, not the viewer's.
6. Cobalt + cream + gold-100 only. No rose anywhere. Rose token reserved for match contexts per BLE-22 / `accent-match`. No amber. No red. State-critical token also avoided on this surface — leaving uses cobalt-300 outline button, not amber.
7. Join / leave iconography symmetric. `door-in` and `door-out` are mirror images at 1.6 stroke. Both surfaced at equal weight on the opt card (44px buttons, side-by-side). Leaving is one tap. No confirmation modal that shames.
8. Empty-state copy non-coercive. "Lingkaran masih sunyi — mulai dengan satu kalimat doa" (verbatim from issue). Sub-line: "Tidak ada tekanan untuk hadir setiap hari." No pressure language ("Bergabunglah!", "Doakan sekarang!") anywhere on the surface.
9. Pray-for-this is opt-in, not required to leave. Detail surface shows two equal-weight buttons: `Tutup` (close) and `Saya doakan` (pray). User may exit without praying. No interstitial. No nag.
