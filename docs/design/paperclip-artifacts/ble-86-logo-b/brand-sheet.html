01 · AnatomyThe mark, taken apart

Every part of the lockup carries meaning. None of them are decorative.

BlessCupıd
  1. Wordmark — set in Source Serif 4, weight 500, optical-sizing 60. The serif is the voice; humanist, breathes.
  2. Cobalt-900 stem — #142447. The deepest cobalt in the system. Not black. Holds gravity without coldness.
  3. Gold halo dot — #C99A3A, replaces the tittle of the i. The single sacred signal. Not a glow, not a ray. Restraint is the point.
  4. Sanctified-ground rule — gold-300 #E9C780, 2px hairline beneath the wordmark, 80% of word width. "Holy ground beneath the name."
  5. Letter-spacing — +1px tracking; the serif breathes wider than UI sans, by design.

08 · Don'tsThings that break the mark

Each violation listed here corresponds to a real visual canon trap dating apps fall into. We are not them.

×
BlessCupıd
Don't stretch or condense

Source Serif 4's optical sizing is calibrated. Stretching breaks the type's structural integrity.

×
BlessCupıd
Don't rotate or skew

The wordmark sits on the sanctified-ground rule. Tilting it breaks the visual covenant.

×
BlessCupıd
Don't recolor in rose

Rose-500 is reserved for matched/connection contexts only. The brand mark in rose reads as a Tinder lockup.

×
BlessCupıd
Don't drop-shadow

Shadows imply Y2K weight or "premium" gloss. The mark holds its own. No bevel, no emboss, no gradient.

×
BC
Don't stained-glass the ground

Religious-kitsch trap. Cream and cobalt are the only grounds. Patterned grounds are forbidden.

×
BlessCupıd
Don't bold or italicize

Weight 500 (Medium) is the locked weight. Bold reads commercial. Italic reads flirty. Neither is on-brand.

Also forbidden (no preview shown — don't render these)

  • Adding a cross, anchor, dove, crown, ichthus, or any iconographic symbol back into the lockup. (Cross-Anchor concept parked for merch only — CEO direction on BLE-22.)
  • Adding stained-glass texture, halo rays, glow, lens flare, or any "consecration shimmer" effect.
  • Replacing the gold halo dot with a heart, flame, ring, or anything that registers as a dating-app trope.
  • Outlining or inlining the wordmark — solid fill only.
  • Combining the wordmark with the BC monogram in a single lockup. Pick one per surface.
  • Using the mark on photographic backgrounds without a cream/cobalt scrim of ≥80% opacity behind it.
