pastor-review

Invite your pastor to walk with you

Pastor mode lets a trusted mentor audit your chats so you can be accountable in your dating journey. You decide what they see and can turn it off any time.

  • You pick who.Only one pastor or mentor at a time. They're invited by email and must accept.
  • They see chats only.Not your matches, not your photos, not your faith profile.
  • You can revoke instantly.One tap turns it off. They're notified the audit ended.

Engineer notes

  • Pastor invite = one active mentor per user. Schema: pastor_link { user_id, mentor_email, status, accepted_at, revoked_at, scope: ['chats'] }. Scope is a list to allow future widening with explicit consent.
  • Matched user is shown a one-time disclosure on first message: "[User] has chosen to be accountable to a pastor. Some of your chats with them may be reviewed for spiritual guidance." Disclosure copy pastor-review blocked.
  • Revocation is instantaneous + notifies the pastor. Audit log is retained for 30 days then purged.

Engineer notes

  • Gesture detection runs at app shell level (React Native StatusBar tap handler). Three taps within 600ms triggers POST /me/panic.
  • Server-side: clear recent_views/recent_searches; set notifications_blocked_until = now + 24h; revoke session token. Discovery hides user from feed for 24h.
  • Emergency-services line is region-aware (i18n). Indonesia: 110. Pull from locale once BLE-46 ships.
