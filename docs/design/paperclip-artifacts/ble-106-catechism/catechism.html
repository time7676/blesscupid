Pernyataan iman BlessCupid

The BlessCupid faith statement

BlessCupid dibangun bagi para pengikut Yesus yang merindukan relasi yang berpusat pada Kristus — persahabatan, komunitas, atau pernikahan.

BlessCupid is built for followers of Jesus seeking a Christ-centered relationship — friendship, community, or marriage.

Kami percaya bahwa setiap orang di sini diciptakan menurut gambar Allah dan layak dihormati (Kejadian 1:27, TB2). Kami menjunjung pernikahan sebagai perjanjian seumur hidup antara seorang laki-laki dan seorang perempuan (Markus 10:6–9, TB2). Kami mengejar kemurnian, kejujuran, dan kelembutan dalam setiap percakapan dan perjumpaan (1 Tesalonika 4:3–7; Efesus 4:29, TB2).

We affirm that every person here is made in God's image and worthy of dignity (Genesis 1:27, NIV). We honor marriage as a lifelong covenant between one man and one woman (Mark 10:6–9, NIV). We pursue purity, honesty, and gentleness in how we speak and connect (1 Thessalonians 4:3–7; Ephesians 4:29, NIV).

"Taruhlah jarimu di sini dan lihatlah tangan-Ku… janganlah engkau tidak percaya lagi, melainkan percayalah."

"Put your finger here; see my hands… Stop doubting and believe."

— Yohanes 20:27, TB2 — John 20:27, NIV

Anda tidak perlu memiliki semua jawaban — pertanyaan yang jujur disambut di sini. Dengan bergabung, Anda berkomitmen untuk menghormati sesama sebagaimana Kristus telah menghormati Anda.

You don't need to have everything figured out — honest questions are welcome here. By joining, you agree to honor others as Christ has honored you.

Constraint checklist — Designer sign-off

Each binding constraint from BLE-106 is met or alternative justified. Pastor passes against this list.

  1. No Hallmark backdrops.

    Ground is cream-50; faith-statement body uses cobalt-900. Hero on screens 01 + 08 is morning-light gradient (cream → gold-100, single radial sun glow), no illustrated angels, doves, or clouds. Met.

  2. No locked-door / gate / seal imagery.

    No padlocks, gates, scrolls-with-seals, or "you-must-agree-to-enter" iconography. Faith-statement card is open-typographic with John 20:27 ("Taruhlah jarimu di sini…") as the hospitality anchor. Met.

  3. VerseCard styling for catechism pull-quote.

    Beat 4 + Beat 8 use identical pattern: gold-100 fill, gold-300 left rule (3px), Source Serif 4, italic reference. Reused on later VOTD card so users learn to recognize scripture by shape. Met.

  4. Acknowledgement checkbox in cobalt-600.

    Beat 3 ack__box is cobalt-600 outline → cobalt-600 fill on check. Amber not used. Met.

  5. "Read full statement" must not bury covenant.

    Beat 3 collapsed preview shows ALL three doctrinal lines (Imago Dei, covenant marriage, chastity-honesty-gentleness) above the ack-checkbox before any expand. Expand is a secondary control, not a gate. Beat 4 is the full read-through (separate beat) that lands when the user opts in. Met.

  6. Touch target ≥ 48pt on checkbox.

    Ack__box is 28×28 visual but the entire ack row (right-side label + helper) is the click target, padded to 48pt min-height. Verified inline. Met.

  7. Reduced-motion fallback.

    Tokens already collapse all --duration-* to 0 on prefers-reduced-motion: reduce. Page also hides decorative sun and disables card transitions. Met.

  8. No coercion language treatments.

    No countdown, no urgency-red, no "agree to continue" CTAs. Primary CTA is cobalt-600 "Saya setuju" / "I agree" — calm. Secondary is ghost "Baca lagi pernyataan lengkap" / "Read full statement again." Tertiary "Belum siap? Kembali" exit always offered without shame. Met.

  9. Welcome value-prop equal weight, hospitality clause not de-ranked.

    Beat 1 stacks all three lines at the same display-lg-equivalent serif weight (22/30, weight 500). Line 3 ("Hati yang jujur disambut — termasuk yang masih bertanya") gets the same visual treatment as lines 1–2 — no smaller, no lighter, no italic-as-aside. Verified at 390×844. Met.
