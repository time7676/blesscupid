# Holy Code of Conduct

**Version:** 1.0
**Pastor blanket-approved:** 2026-04-30 (Julian as proxy)

BlessCupid is a faith-first dating + relationship app for committed Christians. This Code is the spiritual + behavioral standard every user agrees to. It is not a suggestion. It is the spine of the Service.

## §1. Purpose

The Service exists to help Christians pursue marriage, faith-aligned friendship, and community in a way that honors both God and the people you meet.

## §2. Voice

We use words over symbols. We say "Begin a conversation" not heart-icons. We say "Pass quietly" not red X. We say "honor" not "match." Every word in this Code was chosen with care.

## §3. What is welcome

- Real photos of yourself, modestly framed (shoulders + face minimum, no swimwear, no body-only shots).
- Honest answers about your faith tradition, walk stage, and where you are in your spiritual life.
- Marriage-minded intention, or transparency about an alternative (friendship, community).
- Asking about another person's life, walk, family, vocation.
- Pastor-curated conversation prompts when you cannot find your own first line.

## §4. What is not welcome

- **No sexual content.** No nude or sexually-suggestive material in profiles, photos, bios, or chats. We treat the body as a temple.
- **No hookup framing.** This is not Tinder. We are not here for "fun." We are here for covenant.
- **No harassment.** No slurs, no degrading language, no persistent unwanted contact after someone has passed on you.
- **No hate.** No content that demeans a person on the basis of race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, disability, age, faith, or other protected category.
- **No threats or violence.** No threats of harm, including self-harm. Self-harm signals route immediately to support resources.
- **No false identity.** No impersonation, no AI-generated faces, no catfishing.
- **No off-platform pressure.** Do not push someone to switch to another app or share contact details before they are ready.
- **No financial scams.** No solicitation, no fundraising, no sales pitches, no MLM recruiting.
- **No CSAM.** Period. We report to law enforcement immediately.

## §5. Privacy + safety

- We separate your **legal name** (private, used only by safety + moderation team) from your **nickname** (public, what other users see).
- We do not share your email, phone, or exact location with other users, ever.
- Every chat thread has a visible **Report** + **Block** button. Use them.
- Reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

## §6. Two-strike system

| Strike | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Strike 1** | Warning + content removed. You see the violation reason and the relevant section of this Code. |
| **Strike 2** | Account suspended pending review. Other party automatically blocked. |
| **Severe (immediate ban)** | CSAM, doxxing, threats of physical harm, illegal solicitation, repeat-offender re-registration. |

Suspended accounts may appeal once via abuse@blesscupid.com. Repeat appeals are not reviewed.

## §7. Pastor authority

- All UX copy + bio language touching faith, relationships, or moderation is signed off by our Pastor.
- The Pastor may set tradition-specific rules in v1.1 (e.g. a Pastor-curated reading-list per tradition).
- The Pastor's word on a doctrinal moderation question is final. Engineering does not override.

## §8. Honor

The deepest rule: treat every person you meet on BlessCupid as a sister or brother, made in the image of God. If you would not say it to a sister, do not say it to a match. If you would not show it to a brother, do not show it to a match. The Service exists so that something good can grow. Help us keep it that way.
